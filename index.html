Weatherglass

A pocket weather instrument on a black glass dial.

  • Model consensus: ECMWF, GFS, ICON, GEM and JMA side by side, with a confidence score for each.
  • Air and heat: AQI against WHO and Bangladesh limits, plus wet-bulb heat stress and a planner for when it's safe to go out.

Aviary

A field guide to the birds of Bangladesh · বাংলাদেশের পাখি

  • All 828 species recorded in the country, each with photos, names in English, বাংলা and Latin, field notes and the Wikipedia article.
  • Calls and songs for 775 species, with sonograms. A what's around this month view, and a birdwatch log with GPS.
  • The data: my own Python pipeline merges Wikipedia, Wikidata, Commons, xeno-canto and the Wildlife Act 2012 schedule into one offline database. Every photo and recording is credited to its author.

Telegraph

Learn Morse code, then send it by sound, flashlight or vibration.

  • Koch method: 40 lessons at full character speed with Farnsworth spacing. Copy at 90% and the next letter unlocks.
  • Signal lamp: your phone's torch blinks the message out, with a beacon mode and a full-screen flash for night.
  • Straight key: a brass key you tap, decoded live by a decoder that adapts to your speed. The whole app is 1.3 MB.

02 · Web

Platforms you run on your own server

Full-stack web apps with real workflows behind them, open source and self-hostable. No platform owns your data.

Newsroom

A publishing platform built around how editors actually work. A whole newsroom you can run on your own server, not a theme on someone else's platform.

Editorial workflow

Draft, review, publish. Authors submit, editors publish, and an author's piece is locked while it's in review. Unpublishing takes a story off every page at once, and corrections get an "Updated" date.

Roles that protect people

Admin, editor and author roles are enforced on the server. Authors can't see anyone else's work, not even that it exists. There's no public sign-up, and passwords are hashed with Argon2id.

Reader side

A newspaper-style front page, section, author and tag archives, full-text search, RSS, a sitemap, and proper link previews when stories are shared on social media.

English and Bangla

English first. The content model is ready for Bangla, and every date is shown in Dhaka time.

Housemate

The money side of a shared flat: rent, bills, groceries split by who ate how many meals, deposits into a shared fund, and who owes whom. It replaces the notebook and the WhatsApp maths with numbers everyone can check.

Behind it is a real accounting model: every month balances to zero, mistakes are reversed rather than overwritten, and months close properly. The rules live in the database with row-level security and 264 pgTAP tests.

Workshop

Inventory and order management for small workshops and shops: products, customers, orders and stock. Stock can't be edited directly: it's designed around a ledger, so every count can be explained. Money never passes through a float, and every design decision is written down.

Orrery

A desk orrery: the solar system as it is right now in 3D, the Moon's phase, the Sun's day and what's worth looking up at tonight from your city. Run the clock forwards or back at up to a year a second, anywhere from 1800 to 2050.

The ephemeris is my own, with no astronomy libraries, and checked against JPL's DE421: the Sun, Moon and inner planets within 1.5′, rise and set times within 8 seconds.

Cinefile

A self-hostable film library and recommender. Import your Letterboxd history and it recommends films with the reasons shown, tuned to your mood tonight, favouring good films over merely popular ones. Under it is a hybrid model: text embeddings of each film, collaborative filtering trained on MovieLens, and a learned ranker on top, each measured against simple baselines before it ships.

The import, library, film pages and stats work now; the recommender is being built.
